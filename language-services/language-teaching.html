WHO THIS IS FOR

You don't have to feel disconnected from your language anymore.

Diaspora & global learners

Nigerians abroad and foreigners who want to speak Hausa, Fulfulde or Pidgin for family, heritage, travel or work.

Grew up away from home

Nigerians raised in a different region, where the family's own language wasn't spoken at home, who now want to reconnect.

"I understand a little"

LANGUAGES TAUGHT

Full dialect coverage — not just the textbook version.

Hausa

Conversational and formal Hausa, taught the way it's actually spoken.

Fulfulde (Fulani / Pular)

All major dialects covered — Fulani, Fulfulde and Pular — so lessons match the variety your family or community actually speaks.

Simple, one-on-one, at your pace.
