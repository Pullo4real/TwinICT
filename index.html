THE CASE FOR DIGITAL SKILLS

AI may change jobs. The bigger risk is staying unable to work with the change.

A translator, teacher, administrator, marketer, writer, designer or business owner does not have to abandon what they know. The opportunity is to combine that experience with modern tools — and become more capable, not less relevant.

The people who learn to work with technology can often compete for opportunities that were previously out of reach.

Learn by doing

Live coding, practical tasks and projects replace passive learning wherever possible.

Think beyond location

Digital work can connect your skills to clients, employers and audiences far beyond your immediate environment.

Monetise what you know

Explore freelancing, content, language services, digital marketing and other paths around existing strengths.

Keep learning

Technology changes quickly. The goal is not one tool — it is the confidence to keep adapting.
